Step 3 – एक ही वाक्य से 12 उदाहरण

हम एक ही verb “play” से सभी रूप समझेंगे।

🌞 Present – वर्तमान

  1. Simple: I play cricket. – मैं क्रिकेट खेलता हूँ।
  2. Continuous: I am playing cricket. – मैं क्रिकेट खेल रहा हूँ।
  3. Perfect: I have played cricket. – मैं क्रिकेट खेल चुका हूँ।
  4. Perfect Continuous: I have been playing cricket for one hour. – मैं एक घंटे से क्रिकेट खेल रहा हूँ।

🌙 Past – भूतकाल

  1. Simple: I played cricket. – मैंने क्रिकेट खेला।
  2. Continuous: I was playing cricket. – मैं क्रिकेट खेल रहा था।
  3. Perfect: I had played cricket. – मैं क्रिकेट खेल चुका था।
  4. Perfect Continuous: I had been playing cricket for one hour. – मैं एक घंटे से क्रिकेट खेल रहा था।

🚀 Future – भविष्य

  1. Simple: I will play cricket. – मैं क्रिकेट खेलूँगा।
  2. Continuous: I will be playing cricket. – मैं क्रिकेट खेल रहा होऊँगा।
  3. Perfect: I will have played cricket. – मैं क्रिकेट खेल चुका होऊँगा।
  4. Perfect Continuous: I will have been playing cricket for one hour. – मैं एक घंटे से क्रिकेट खेल रहा होऊँगा।

Step 4 – Negative और Question कैसे बनाएँ?

Simple Present: He plays. → He does not play. → Does he play?

Simple Past: He played. → He did not play. → Did he play?

Future: He will play. → He will not play. → Will he play?

बहुत जरूरी: does/did के बाद Verb की पहली form (V1) आती है: Does he play? Did she go? (plays/went नहीं)।

Has: He/She/It/एक नाम के साथ। Have: I/You/We/They के साथ। Had: सभी के साथ (Past Perfect)।

Since और For

Since = कब से?

since Monday, since 2020, since 8 AM

For = कितने समय से?

for two hours, for three days, for a year

अपना अभ्यास जाँचें

  1. She ___ to school every day. (go/goes)
  2. They ___ playing now. (is/are)
  3. I ___ my work yesterday. (finish/finished)
  4. He has ___ his food. (eat/eaten)
  5. We ___ visit Raipur tomorrow. (will/were)

उत्तर: goes, are, finished, eaten, will.

याद रखने का मंत्र

पहले समय पहचानो – Present / Past / Future। फिर काम का रूप पहचानो – Simple / Continuous / Perfect / Perfect Continuous। उसके बाद table से formula चुनो और वाक्य बनाओ।
